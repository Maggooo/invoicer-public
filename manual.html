User Manual

Everything Invoice Cove can do, screen by screen.

Invoice Cove runs entirely on your device — invoices, quotes, customers and your business profile are stored locally, not on any Invoice Cove server. We don't collect, see, or receive any data about your invoices or how you use the app — there's no analytics, no tracking, nothing sent anywhere in the background. The only time the app needs an internet connection at all is to process a one-time Invoice Cove Pro purchase through Google Play. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Before you uninstall the app or clear its storage: that data is not backed up anywhere by us. Uninstalling Invoice Cove, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, permanently deletes every invoice, quote, customer and setting on this device — there's no cloud copy to restore from. Export what you need as PDF, CSV or ZIP first (see Managing invoices).

1 Home screen

The Home screen is your starting point, with one tile for each main action: New Quote, Quotes, New Invoice, Invoices, New Customer, Customers, Calendar, and Reports. Tap any tile to jump straight there.

The gear icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner of most screens opens Business Details & Settings.

2 Customers

Add a customer from the Customers tab (tap the + icon) or inline while creating an invoice/quote. Fill in a name and any of the optional fields — address, email, phone, tax ID, VAT number, and a color to tint their documents.

Saving works differently here than on invoices. A customer has its own "Save Customer" (or "Save Changes" when editing) button — it's saved the moment you tap it, before you even leave the sheet. If you try to close the form with unsaved changes, Invoice Cove asks you to confirm first.

3 Creating an invoice

From New Invoice: pick a customer (or create one inline), set the invoice date and due date, add one or more line items (description, quantity, unit price), and optionally a tax rate, a discount, and notes.

Preview renders a throwaway PDF so you can check how it looks — nothing is saved yet.

There's no separate "Save" button. Tapping Generate Invoice does three things at once: it saves the invoice, creates the PDF, and opens your device's share sheet so you can send it. If you leave the New Invoice screen before tapping Generate, everything you entered is discarded — Invoice Cove will ask you to confirm before discarding.

4 Managing invoices

The Invoices tab groups your invoices into a folder per customer. Open a folder to see, view, re-share, mark paid, void, or delete individual invoices. From the folder list you can also export invoices as a CSV spreadsheet or a ZIP of PDFs, or archive a single customer's folder as a ZIP.

5 Creating a quote

New Quote works exactly like New Invoice — same fields, same Preview button, same "Generate Quote does save + PDF + share in one tap" behavior — just with a Quote Date and a Valid Until date instead of an invoice date and due date.

6 Managing quotes & converting to invoice

The Quotes tab lists your quotes by customer, the same way Invoices does. Open a quote to view or share it, mark it Accepted or Declined, record a deposit, or delete it.

Once a customer accepts a quote, use Convert to Invoice to turn the checked line items into a real, independently-editable invoice — the original quote is marked Converted and kept for your records.

7 Calendar & reminders

The Calendar tab shows due dates and any notes/reminders you've added, so you can see what's coming up at a glance. Invoice Cove can also send you a local reminder notification for invoices approaching their due date.

8 Reports

Reports gives you a quick overview of total revenue, outstanding and overdue amounts, monthly revenue, and your top customers by billed total.

9 Business details & settings

Open Settings from the gear icon to set your business name, address, logo, and payment details (shown on every invoice/quote), your default due-date offset, your preferred tax label, and to clear the item-description autocomplete memory.

This is also where you'll find App Lock, if you'd like Invoice Cove to require a PIN when it's freshly launched.

10 PDF templates

Invoice Cove ships with a set of PDF template designs, from clean and minimal to bold and organic. Pick one from the Template Picker (in Settings) — it applies to every invoice and quote you generate from then on.

11 Free plan vs. Invoice Cove Pro

Invoice Cove is free to use, with a few sensible limits:

  • Up to 3 invoices and 3 quotes generated per calendar month
  • Up to 3 customers at a time
  • Invoice/quote numbers are auto-assigned, not editable
  • CSV and ZIP export are Pro-only

Invoice Cove Pro is a single one-time purchase through Google Play — not a subscription — that removes every one of those limits for good. See the Terms of Use for the full details.
