Everything a small business needs to get paid

From the first quote to the paid invoice, all in one app on your phone.

Invoices in one tap

Pick a customer, add your items, tax and discount. Generate Invoice saves it, creates the PDF and opens the share sheet. Unfinished invoices are kept as drafts automatically.

Quotes that become invoices

Send a quote, mark it accepted or declined, then use Convert to Invoice to turn the chosen items into a real invoice.

18 PDF templates

From Classic and Minimal to Vintage Garage and Blush Botanical. Set a default, or choose a different one for a single document.

Know who has paid

Every invoice is Unpaid, Overdue, Partially Paid, Paid or Void. Reports show revenue, what is outstanding and your top customers.

Reminders and calendar

Get notified when an invoice is due soon or overdue, and keep your own notes with reminders in a monthly calendar.

13 languages

English, Română, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, Polski, Čeština, Slovenčina, Magyar, Nederlands and Svenska. Your PDFs follow the app's language.

A look for every kind of business

A few of the 18 PDF templates, all showing the same invoice. Every template prints only the details you filled in.

Your business data is yours

Invoice Cove runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, see or receive anything about your invoices or how you use the app.

  • No accountOpen the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No cloudInvoices, quotes and customers are stored on your phone only.
  • No trackingNo analytics, nothing sent anywhere in the background.
  • App LockA PIN or your fingerprint protects the app from the first launch.
Because nothing is stored in the cloud, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes your data. Use Backup & Restore (Pro) or export what you need. See the User Guide.
