Invoice Cove and Invoice Simple compared: what you pay, what is free, and where your data lives.

Where Invoice Simple is the better choice

Invoice Simple does a lot that Invoice Cove does not. Choose it if you need:

  • an iPhone app, or invoicing from a computer in a web browser
  • card payments collected online through the invoice
  • your invoices synced between devices through an online account
  • extras such as client signatures, photos attached to invoices and notifications when an invoice is read

Where Invoice Cove is the better choice

  • You do not want a subscription. Pro is paid once through Google Play and never renews.
  • You want unlimited invoices, quotes and customers without paying every month. Pro unlocks all of them for a single payment. If you only send a few, the Free plan includes 3 invoices and 3 quotes every month, with no time limit.
  • You want your business data off the cloud. There is no account, and invoices, quotes and customers stay on your phone.
  • You often work without signal. Everything except sending works offline. See how the offline app works.
  • You or your customers prefer another language. Invoice Cove and its PDFs are available in 13 languages.

Switching from Invoice Simple

Invoice Cove cannot import data from other apps, so you add your customers and items once. If you want to carry on from your current invoice number, Pro lets you edit invoice and quote numbers. Keep your old app until you no longer need its records.
