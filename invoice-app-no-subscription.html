An invoice app with no subscription

Invoice Cove is free to use. If you need more, Pro is a single one-time purchase. No monthly fee, ever.

Get it on Google Play

Most invoice apps charge every month or every year, and stop working the way you need the moment you stop paying. If you send a handful of invoices a month, that adds up to a lot of money for a small job. Invoice Cove works differently.

How Invoice Cove is paid for

There are only two options, and neither one renews:

  • Free: no cost and no time limit. It is not a trial that runs out.
  • Invoice Cove Pro: one payment through Google Play. You pay once and Pro stays unlocked for as long as you use the app.

Nothing renews in the background and there is nothing to cancel. The purchase is tied to the Google account you bought it with, so if you reinstall the app or move to a new phone with the same account, Pro comes back.

What you get for free

  • 3 invoices and 3 quotes every month
  • Up to 3 customers at a time
  • All PDF templates and all 13 languages
  • Unlimited drafts, previews and PDF sharing
  • App Lock with a PIN or your fingerprint

The monthly limits reset at the start of each calendar month. For a sole trader with a few regular clients, Free is often enough.

What the one-time Pro purchase adds

  • Unlimited invoices, quotes and customers
  • Editable invoice and quote numbers, so you can carry on from your old numbering
  • Excel, CSV and ZIP export for your accountant
  • Full Backup & Restore, to move everything to a new phone

Why no subscription?

A subscription makes sense when a company runs servers for you: cloud storage, sync between devices, online payments. Invoice Cove does none of that. Your invoices, quotes and customers are stored on your phone, and we do not run a server that holds them. With no ongoing cost on our side, there is no reason to charge you an ongoing fee.

That also means some things are not included. There is no web version, no sync between devices and no online card payments. If you need those, a cloud invoicing service with a subscription is the better choice. If you want to create a professional invoice on your phone and send it, you do not need to pay every month for that.

Questions

Is there a free trial that turns into a subscription?

No. There is no trial and no subscription. Free stays free, and Pro is paid once.

Do I need an account to buy Pro?

Only your Google Play account, which you already use to install apps. Invoice Cove itself has no accounts.

What happens when I change phones?

Pro comes back automatically when you install the app with the same Google account. To bring your invoices, quotes and customers with you, use Backup & Restore: it saves everything into one file that you restore on the new phone. See Backup & restore in the User Guide.
