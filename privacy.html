Privacy Policy

Invoice Cove - Offline Billing

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Invoice Cove is built to run entirely on your device. There is no Invoice Cove server, no account to create, and no analytics or advertising SDK inside the app. This page explains exactly what that means in practice.

No account required No analytics No ads No data sold, ever

1. Your business data stays on your device

Every invoice, quote, customer record, business profile, and generated PDF you create in Invoice Cove is stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone. We — Magotel, the developer of Invoice Cove — never receive a copy of it, don't have a server it could be sent to, and can't see it.

We don't collect any data about your invoices, quotes, or customers, and we don't collect any data about how you use the app either — no invoice totals, no customer names, no usage analytics, no crash reports, no device identifiers sent to us. Nothing about your activity in Invoice Cove is transmitted anywhere in the background.

2. We're not responsible for data that only ever existed on your device

Because your invoices, quotes, customer records and settings are stored solely on your phone and never on any server we control, Magotel has no ability to see, back up, or recover that data — and no responsibility for it if it's lost. If you uninstall Invoice Cove, clear its storage, lose your phone, or your device is reset, that data is gone unless you personally exported or backed it up beforehand (see Managing invoices for how to export). This also means we can't restore anything on your behalf if something goes wrong locally on your device — there's simply nothing on our end to restore it from.

3. Sharing a PDF is your choice, each time

When you tap Share on an invoice or quote, Android's own share sheet opens and you pick the destination — email, messaging apps, cloud storage, whatever you have installed. Invoice Cove doesn't send anything automatically or in the background. Once you've shared a file with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to what happens to it next, not this one.

4. Choosing a logo image

Adding a business logo uses your device's built-in photo picker. Invoice Cove only receives the single image you explicitly select — it does not get broader access to your photo library.

5. Exporting invoices as CSV or ZIP

Exporting invoices opens Android's standard "Save As" dialog, where you choose exactly where the file is saved on your device. Invoice Cove doesn't have open-ended access to your device storage.

6. Local reminders

Invoice Cove can remind you about upcoming due dates using local notifications scheduled on your device. These are not push notifications — nothing is sent to or received from a remote server to make them work.

7. Invoice Cove Pro (one-time purchase)

Unlocking Invoice Cove Pro is a one-time, non-subscription purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We use the Google Play Billing Library to complete and verify the purchase — this is the one feature in the app that requires an internet connection, solely to talk to Google Play's own servers. Your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) are handled by Google, not by us; we never see or store them. Google's own Privacy Policy applies to that transaction.

8. Android system backups

If you have Android's built-in "Back up to Google Drive" setting turned on at the system level, your device may include Invoice Cove's local app data in that standard OS backup. That's a feature of Android itself, not something Invoice Cove initiates or has access to — we don't receive anything from it, and we can't guarantee it will restore your data correctly.

9. Children's privacy

Invoice Cove is a business tool and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect any data from children, in line with the rest of this policy: we don't collect data from anyone.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and post the revised version at this same address.
